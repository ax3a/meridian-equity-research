Item 1. Business

Acme Corp designs, manufactures and sells widgets for the industrial automation market. The Company was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Springfield. Acme sells its products through a direct sales force and a network of independent distributors across North America, Europe and Asia. The Company's fiscal year ends on December 31.

Acme's primary product lines include the WidgetPro series, the WidgetLite series, and a growing services business that provides installation, maintenance and analytics software to industrial customers. Management believes the services business will continue to grow faster than the core hardware business over the next several years.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

The following discussion should be read together with our consolidated financial statements and the related notes included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. Net sales increased 12% year over year, driven primarily by growth in the services business and modest price increases across the WidgetPro product line. Gross margin expanded slightly as a result of favorable product mix and lower freight costs compared with the prior year.

Operating expenses increased due to continued investment in research and development and higher headcount in our sales organization as we expanded into new geographic markets. Management expects these investments to support continued revenue growth over the medium term, although near-term profitability may be affected.
